A New tab's search now fills the page with what it finds. Start typing and **Open** and **Recents** give way to matching pages, databases, canvases, tools, open tabs and a web search, right under the field; Escape brings them back. Every page shows its Status, so active work stands out, and a match inside a page shows the sentence it came from. Before you type, press ↓ to move from the field into **Recents** and Enter to open one. **Open page…** is no longer a New tab tool, since the search opens pages directly; it stays in the panel's `+` menu.
