Agents now leave problems they notice outside their task in your Inbox, so they are not lost. A
defect, a recurring friction or a better long-term design an Agent finds while working arrives as
a message from its chat, with its evidence and a suggested direction, ready to hand to another
chat. The Agent keeps its current change to the task. It sends one only when the finding matters
beyond the task and is not already tracked, so most chats send none.
