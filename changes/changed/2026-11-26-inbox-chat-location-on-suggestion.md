Choose where an Inbox chat works right on its suggestion. The menu beside **Start chat** now holds **Use a worktree** under the model, so one message can start in a worktree or in your checkout without rewriting its dispatch. The decision shows **Worktree** or **Local** whenever a message differs from your Inbox setting, its tooltip says where the chat will work, and **Set as Inbox default** keeps both choices for every new Inbox chat. A chat without a Project, or in a folder that is not a Git repository, is no longer offered a worktree it cannot use.
