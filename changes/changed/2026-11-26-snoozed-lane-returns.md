The sidebar's Snoozed list now says when each chat comes back. In Attention view, a snoozed chat ends its row with its return: a time today, **Tomorrow**, a weekday or a date. Chats returning after tomorrow fold behind one quiet row, such as `2 later · Mon – Dec 8`, until you open it. **View options** now stays visible beside the first list's heading, and its new **Previews** sets how many lines rows in Pinned, Active and Working give to what each chat is about: **Two lines**, **One line** or **Off**. Snoozed and settled rows keep to one line.
