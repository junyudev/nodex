Every picture an Agent looks at now shows right on the step that brought it back, whichever Agent and tool it was. A file it viewed reads as **Viewed** and the file's name, a screenshot a script or browser tool takes sits beside that step, and a folded run of work shows its pictures on its summary line, on the desktop and on your phone. Hover a picture for a closer look, or open it to move through all of them full size.
