Insights now opens on your Skyline and Receipt cards, ready to share. Both follow the period and agents you pick on the page. Hover a card to copy it, save it as a PNG or post it on X, and add your name, hide the cost or switch between dark and light cards right below them. Both cards name the agents they counted and show `npx nodex-card`, so anyone who sees yours can make their own, and the Receipt leads with what your usage is worth at API list price.
