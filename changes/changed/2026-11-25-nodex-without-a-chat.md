Nodex no longer keeps a hidden conversation of its own: each Inbox suggestion and each event
Automation assessment now runs on its own, with only what that decision needs, so suggestions
arrive faster and cost far less. In Activity, **How Nodex decided** opens the run behind any of
its decisions. Work that finished a turn waiting on you now shows **Needs your reply** in the
Inbox and on its card, and reviews, problems and replies notify you directly. **Open Nodex** and
**Start Nodex fresh** are gone, as are Nodex's notes on work and the **Managed by Nodex** chat
option.
