A narrow Inbox, such as one beside a Page or Aerial view, no longer stacks its views under its
tools, where clicking **Activity** could open the Board instead. As the Inbox narrows, its header
gathers its tools into **More actions** and then shows views by their icons, keeping the current
view's name longest. A suggestion that no longer fits beside a message's icons moves under them,
and **How the Inbox plans** moves each label above its value.
