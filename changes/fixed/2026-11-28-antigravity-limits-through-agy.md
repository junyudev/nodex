Reading your Antigravity limits no longer risks your Google account. Nodex now asks `agy` for them,
the same answer `/usage` gives in a terminal, instead of calling Google's internal quota service
with your sign-in, which Google may flag. Limits are read only while the Antigravity usage meter is
on: every five minutes while Agents run, when you open its card, and when you turn the meter on.
Turning it off in **Settings > Appearance > Usage meters** now stops every read.
