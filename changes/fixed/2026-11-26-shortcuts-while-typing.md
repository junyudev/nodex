Chat and panel shortcuts now work while you are typing in a composer, the Inbox or a Page:
`⌘N` starts a new chat, `⌥⌘↓` / `⌥⌘↑` move between chats, and archive, pin, unread, settle,
snooze, side chat, Browser tab, Files and Review all respond without clicking away first. Dialogs,
menus and Terminals keep the keys they use, and AltGr or input-method typing never triggers a
shortcut.
