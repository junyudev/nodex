Mini keeps your chats in view over any app and starts a new one in a few keystrokes. Press **Shift+Option+Space** anywhere to type a request: Enter starts a chat in the Project you picked, and **Cmd+Enter** saves it to your Inbox instead. Choose **Show Mini** in the account menu to keep a small dock at the edge of your screen with a card for each chat that needs you, failed, finished or is working; you can answer a question, approve, accept a review, reply or stop right on the card, and drag Mini to any corner or edge. Using Mini never pulls Nodex forward; it steps aside while you are in Nodex, and while it shows a chat it stands in for that chat's system notifications. **Settings > General > Mini** chooses whether it shows only while something is happening or always.
