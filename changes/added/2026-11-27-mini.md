Mini keeps your chats in view over any app and starts a new one in a few keystrokes. While anything is happening, a small dock rests at the edge of your screen with a card for each chat that needs you, failed, finished or is working, and you can answer a question, approve, accept a review, reply or stop right on the card. Press **Shift+Option+Space** to type a request: Enter starts a chat in the Project you picked, and **Cmd+Enter** saves it to your Inbox instead. Opening Mini never pulls Nodex forward; it steps aside while you are in Nodex, and while it shows a chat it stands in for that chat's system notifications. Choose when it shows in **Settings > General > Mini**, and drag it to any corner or edge.
