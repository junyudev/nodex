Mini keeps your chats in view over any app and starts a new one in a few keystrokes. Press **Shift+Option+Space** anywhere to type a request: Enter starts a chat in the Project you picked, and **Cmd+Enter** saves it to your Inbox instead. Choose **Show Mini** in the account menu to keep a small dock on your screen with a card for each chat that needs you, failed, finished or is working. You can answer a question, approve, accept a review, reply or stop right on the card, and drag Mini anywhere. Hold ⌥ when dropping it to snap to a corner or edge. Using Mini never pulls Nodex forward, and it stays where you put it while you work in Nodex too. While Mini shows a chat, it stands in for that chat's system notifications. **Settings > General > Mini** chooses whether it shows only while something is happening or always. When an agent uses your computer, **Send to Mini** on its live preview docks the previews beside Mini so you can watch from any app. Type `/mini` in a chat to show or hide it.
