Arrange the sidebar by attention. The toggle beside Search (`⌥⌘U`) lists **Pinned** chats, then **Active** chats waiting on you, **Working** chats whose Agents are busy and **Snoozed** chats, and **View options** beside the first list chooses which lists show and whether they cover every Project or just one. Each row names its Project and previews what came back, and a chat whose Page waits on you says so first, such as **Ready for your review**. Rows hold still while you point at the sidebar, `⌥⌘↑` and `⌥⌘↓` move between chats, and `⌥⌘A` jumps to the next one that needs you.
