A chat now shows what its Agent left in your Inbox, and you can hand it straight back. Each
follow-up appears beneath the reply that raised it and under **Follow-ups** in the chat's summary,
with where it stands now: waiting, sent to a chat, in Todo, or done. **Continue here** gives it to
the same chat, and **Fork** starts a fork of that chat to work on it with the same context, from
the chat or from the Inbox.
